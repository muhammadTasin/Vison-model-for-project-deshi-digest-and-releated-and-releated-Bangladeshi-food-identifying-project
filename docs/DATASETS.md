# Dataset provenance and permission

## Stage‑4

Stage‑4 used multiple Bangladeshi food datasets together with PithaNet. PithaNet was used with direct permission from its author(s). The raw PithaNet dataset, raw training images, dataset archives, manifests, and private permission correspondence are intentionally not redistributed here.

The recorded Stage‑4 population is 4,152 clean unique PithaNet training images plus 2,720 existing-food rehearsal samples, for 6,872 training samples; validation has 996 held-out images.

Anyone reproducing the work must independently obtain lawful access, comply with each dataset’s terms, retain provenance records, and keep local absolute paths and manifests out of public Git history.

## Historical Stage‑3 evidence

The previous Stage‑3 documentation recorded public sources as considered or prepared rather than independently verified inclusion. Those historical claims remain conservative: raw data is not redistributed and future reproduction requires an audit of license, source, deduplication, label mapping, and split membership.
